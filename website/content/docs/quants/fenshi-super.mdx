**Fenshi Super** marks minute-level **dip / strong-buy / high-sell / run** points from intraday geometry (not an L2 capital gate). **Signals only** — no position ledger or fill write-back.

<Callout title="How to open" type="info">
**Not subscribed**: product page subscribe panel → checkout.  
**Already subscribed**: **My subscriptions** → **Fenshi Super** → **Enter** (see [Getting started](/docs/quants/getting-started)).
</Callout>

<Callout title="Separate from Fenshi Leader" type="info">
Independent engines, watch slots, and SKUs. Points never cross between the two products.
</Callout>

## Who it is for

- Self-directed traders who want multi-symbol intraday structure
- Users who prefer on-page toast + sound over Feishu
- Users who do not need base-lot bookkeeping

## Before you start

1. Complete [Getting started](/docs/quants/getting-started)
2. Have an active watch-slot plan
3. Prepare symbol codes / names / pinyin

## Open the workbench

1. Open **My subscriptions**
2. Find **Fenshi Super** under Specialty · PATTERN
3. Click **Enter**

Or open the [product page](https://www.alphaio.vip/features/fenshi_super).

## Subscribe watch slots

1. Pick a monthly concurrent-watch tier on the product page
2. Finish checkout
3. Confirm the slot bar (can watch N · selected M) and expiry

One slot = one live chart card. Extra symbols can stay listed but cards beyond quota are greyed out and paused.

## Add / replace symbols

1. Type code, name, or pinyin in **Add symbol**
2. Pick from the dropdown
3. The symbol joins the watchlist

![Add symbol: pinyin search](/docs/assets/quants/fenshi-add-symbol.png)

**When the slot is full:** remove symbols you no longer need so “selected” is below “can watch”, then add new ones. You cannot keep more live cards than your quota.

## Watch UI

| Control | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Add symbol | Search and add |
| Grid layout | Cards per screen |
| Alerts | On-page toast |
| Sound | Buy/sell tones |
| Slot bar | Quota, selected count, expiry |
| Every 15s / Refresh | Polling |

Each card shows name/code, last price, high/low, price/avg/prev-close lines, point markers, and a short reason.

![Workbench: dual cards and high-sell points](/docs/assets/quants/fenshi-workbench.png)

## Reading points

| Point | Rough meaning (structure hint, not an order) |
| --- | --- |
| **Dip** | Weakness vs average, then stabilize |
| **Strong-buy** | Strength above average / breakout-style structure |
| **High-sell** | Stall after a push higher |
| **Run** | Accelerating weakness / caution |

No share counts or positions. Similar wording may appear in Smart-T Feishu cards, but this product is **web-watch only** — no Feishu, no base lot.

## Alerts and sound

With **Alerts** / **Sound** on, new points toast and use different tones.

<Callout title="No Feishu push" type="info">
Web toast and sound only. For Feishu follow alerts use [Smart-T Engine](/docs/quants/follow-t-assist).
</Callout>

## Daily workflow

1. Enter from My subscriptions; keep selected ≤ quota
2. Watch the grid; check the card when alerted
3. Place orders yourself
4. Review markers after close; no base-lot book

## FAQ

**Grey cards?** Selected exceeds concurrent quota — remove symbols or upgrade.  
**Vs Leader?** Leader adds L2 gates; Super does not. Buy separately.  
**No Feishu?** Correct — that is Smart-T.  
**No new points?** Check login/entitlement and Refresh; quiet days are normal.

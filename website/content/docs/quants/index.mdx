These manuals cover specialty tools on the **AlphaIO Portal** ([www.alphaio.vip](https://www.alphaio.vip/)), not the AI console at `ai.alphaio.vip`.

<Callout title="Research tools only" type="warn">
Signals and reminders are for research and self-managed decisions. They are not investment advice and the platform does not place orders for you.
</Callout>

## Two products in this guide

| Product | Product code | Slot meaning | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Fenshi Super](/docs/quants/fenshi-super) | `fenshi_super` | **Watch slots** — how many symbols you can monitor at once | Web chart (~15s refresh), toast + sound |
| [Smart-T Engine](/docs/quants/follow-t-assist) | `follow_t_assist` | **Follow slots** — how many legs you can track at once | Server minute signals + **Feishu** cards; you execute |

Both are independent SKUs. Fenshi Super has **no position ledger**; Smart-T registers a base lot for alerts and review.

## How to open a product

- **Not subscribed yet**: feature hall or product page → pick a monthly slot plan → checkout
- **Already subscribed**: top nav **My subscriptions** → **Enter** on that row

Details: [Getting started](/docs/quants/getting-started).

## Suggested path

<Steps>
<Step>

### Account

Follow [Getting started](/docs/quants/getting-started): invite-code register, verify email, sign in.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick a product

- Multi-symbol charts and on-page alerts → [Fenshi Super](/docs/quants/fenshi-super)
- Feishu push, base-lot tracking, signal review → [Smart-T Engine](/docs/quants/follow-t-assist)

</Step>
<Step>

### Subscribe, then configure

- Fenshi Super: buy watch slots → open workbench → add symbols
- Smart-T: **configure Feishu and test first** → add symbols / register base lots → use Feishu for alerts and the console for review

</Step>
</Steps>

[https://www.alphaio.vip/login](https://www.alphaio.vip/login?callbackUrl=%2Fhome)

Product intros can be opened anonymously; the **watch / Smart-T console** needs sign-in.

## Which entry to use

| Your state | Path |
| --- | --- |
| No account yet | Invite register → verify email → sign in |
| Signed in, not subscribed | Feature hall or product page → checkout |
| Already subscribed | **My subscriptions** → **Enter** (fastest) |

## Register with invite code

Portal registration is **email + invite code only**. Contact support / ops if you do not have a code.

1. Open the [login page](https://www.alphaio.vip/login?callbackUrl=%2Fhome)
2. Switch to **Invite registration**
3. Fill in:
   - **Invite code** (required): example `INVITE-XXXX-XXXX`
   - **Email** (required)
   - **Password** (required): at least 6 characters
   - **Nickname** (optional)
4. Accept the Service Use and Risk Disclosure Agreement
5. Click **Register**
6. Verify email, then sign in

Use “No invite code? Contact support” under the form, or reach ops directly.

![Invite registration page](/docs/assets/quants/portal-register.png)

### Registration notes

- **Email verification is required** before normal sign-in
- Check spam or resend verification if the mail is missing
- Sign-in verifies entitlements; it does not change after-hours data definitions

## Sign in

1. Open [https://www.alphaio.vip/login](https://www.alphaio.vip/login?callbackUrl=%2Fhome)
2. Keep the **Login** tab selected
3. Enter **email** and **password**
4. Optionally keep **Stay signed in**
5. Click **Login** (usually lands on home)

Forgot password: use **Forgot password?** and follow the email.

![Login page](/docs/assets/quants/portal-login.png)

## Open from My subscriptions (recommended when entitled)

1. After sign-in, open **My subscriptions** (home list, often [https://www.alphaio.vip/home](https://www.alphaio.vip/home))
2. Find **Specialty · PATTERN**
3. Click **Enter** on:
   - **Smart-T Engine** (Details / Renew may also appear)
   - **Fenshi Super**

Status shows as active with an expiry date.

![My subscriptions: Smart-T and Fenshi Super](/docs/assets/quants/portal-subscriptions.png)

## Open from the feature hall (not subscribed yet)

1. Go to [Feature hall](https://www.alphaio.vip/features)
2. Filter specialty tools:  
   [https://www.alphaio.vip/features?domain=pattern](https://www.alphaio.vip/features?domain=pattern)
3. Open:
   - [Fenshi Super](https://www.alphaio.vip/features/fenshi_super)
   - [Smart-T Engine](https://www.alphaio.vip/features/follow_t_assist)

Guests can read the intro; after login you can subscribe and use the workbench.

## Subscribe slots

On the product page, pick a **monthly slot tier** and finish checkout:

- Fenshi Super → **watch slots**
- Smart-T Engine → **follow slots**

Then use **Enter** on My subscriptions.

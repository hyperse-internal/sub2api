---
title: Create an account
description: Create an AlphaIO account and sign in so you can top up and connect tools.
---

After you register and sign in at [ai.alphaio.vip](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login), you can open the console to top up, create API Keys, and more.

## Sign up

1. Open the login page: [https://ai.alphaio.vip/login](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login)
2. Click **Sign up** at the bottom of the page
3. In the **Create account** form, fill in:
   - **Email**: used for login and recovery
   - **Password**: at least 6 characters
   - **Confirm password**: must match the password
   - **Promo code** (optional): fill in only if you have one
4. Click **Continue** to finish registration

You can also click **GitHub** or **Google** to create an account with a third-party provider.

![Sign-up page](/docs/assets/register.png)

## Sign in

If you already have an account, open the same URL [https://ai.alphaio.vip/login](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login):

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
2. Click **Sign in**

**GitHub** / **Google** quick sign-in is also supported. If you do not have an account yet, click **Sign up** at the bottom of the page.

![Sign-in page](/docs/assets/login.png)

## Next steps

After you sign in:

1. Go to [Top up or redeem](/docs/purchase) to get balance
2. Create an API Key, then follow [Configure tools](/docs/config-tool) to connect clients

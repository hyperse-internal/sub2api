After you register at [ai.alphaio.vip](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login), you can open the console to top up, create API Keys, and more.

## Steps

1. Open the login page: [https://ai.alphaio.vip/login](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login)
2. Click **Sign up** at the bottom of the page
3. In the **Create account** form, fill in:
   - **Email**: used for login and recovery
   - **Password**: at least 6 characters
   - **Confirm password**: must match the password
   - **Promo code** (optional): fill in only if you have one
4. Click **Continue** to finish registration

You can also click **GitHub** or **Google** to create an account with a third-party provider.

![Sign-up page](/docs/assets/register.png)

## Next steps

1. Go to [Sign in](/docs/account/login) to open the console
2. Get balance via [Top up](/docs/billing/topup) or [Redeem](/docs/billing/redeem)
3. Create an API Key, then follow [Tool setup](/docs/tools/cc-switch) to connect clients

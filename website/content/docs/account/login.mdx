title: Sign in
icon: LogIn
description: Sign in to the AlphaIO console with email/password or a third-party account.
---

If you already have an account, open [https://ai.alphaio.vip/login](https://ai.alphaio.vip/login):

1. Enter your **Email** and **Password**
2. Click **Sign in**

**GitHub** / **Google** quick sign-in is also supported. If you do not have an account yet, click **Sign up** at the bottom of the page, or follow [Sign up](/docs/account/register).

![Sign-in page](/docs/assets/login.png)

## Next steps

After you sign in:

1. Go to [Top up](/docs/billing/topup) or [Redeem](/docs/billing/redeem) to get balance
2. Create an API Key, then follow [Tool setup](/docs/tools/cc-switch) to connect clients
